The snapshot

Short questions, straight answers, a clear picture

Opinions about AI are easy to collect and hard to use. What a person does with their hours is harder to get and far more useful. Every question here asks for the work itself: what repeats, what drains, what gets postponed, and what stays private.

What came back

The shape of the week

Messages first

The day opens on KingsChat and the messages set the pace. Whatever else is planned, the inbox decides the first hour.

On site and in review

The week runs on construction site visits and review meetings across several projects, one after another.

Work made of decisions

Creative direction, building, engineering and enterprise in one role. The real work is judgement: designs, approvals, problem solving.

Time lost searching

Not waiting and not app switching. Searching is named as the biggest leak, which points at findability rather than speed.

Reading is the drag

Bulky documents are the task most likely to be pushed to tomorrow, and typing on a phone is the daily friction.

A clear privacy line

Financials must never leave personal control. That line is set before any tool is chosen, not after.
