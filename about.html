About

What this snapshot is for

A short set of questions that captures how a person's week is actually spent, so any help offered rests on facts rather than assumptions.

Purpose

Facts about the week, not opinions about AI

Questions about artificial intelligence tend to collect opinions that go stale in a month. Questions about the work last longer. The questionnaire asks what repeats through the day, what repeats through the week, which task takes the most thinking, where the time leaks, what is typed most, what gets postponed, and what must never leave personal control.

The answers are then read against one standard: which of these tasks can be carried without the person losing control of the decision. Everything on this site follows from that.

The questionnaire

The 13 questions

Answered in your own words, with real numbers where possible. About ten minutes.

1. Repeated daily

What are the most repeated things you do through your day?

2. Repeated weekly

What are the most repeated things you do through your week?

3. Heaviest thinking

What is the most mentally demanding task you perform?

4. The role

Describe your job in one sentence.

5. Strengths

What skills help you thrive in what you do?

6. Senses

Which senses do you rely on most? Sight, touch, hearing, speech?

7. Time leaks

Where do you lose the most time? Waiting, searching, or switching between apps?

8. Writing volume

What do you write or type most often, and how many a week?

9. Postponed work

Which tasks do you dread, or keep pushing to tomorrow?

10. Repeated decisions

What decisions do you repeat from the same kind of information?

11. Daily apps

Which apps do you use daily, and which one frustrates you?

12. The privacy line

What must never leave your control or be shared with a tool?

13. Five free hours

If you gained five hours a week, where would those hours go?

Collection

How the answers get back

The questionnaire is a single page that works on any phone. When it is submitted, the answers go straight to the organiser's collection point and are read there. The respondent does not need an account, a download or anything installed, and nothing extra is collected beyond the answers and the name given.

Privacy note

Anything named as private in the answers stays private. Names are withheld from this site: every submission is published anonymously, identified only by a number and the date it arrived, with no city or country shown. Financial information in particular never goes into a tool, and no reply, document or approval goes out in anyone's name without their own decision to send it. This page is public and is taken down on request.

Questions about the snapshot, or a request to have a response removed, come through the same channel the questionnaire arrived on.
