The 13 questions and the answers

Everything on this page came in through the questionnaire form in one sitting. Each answer sits under the question that produced it.

01
Respondent 1
Anonymous submission · Creative Director, builder, engineer and entrepreneur · Response received 20 September 2026
  • Repeated work
  • Heavy thinking
  • Where time leaks
  • Privacy line
  • Five free hours
1What are the most repeated things you do through your day?
Respond to messages on KingsChat.
The day starts in the message queue, so the first hour is reactive rather than chosen.
2What are the most repeated things you do through your week?
Visit the construction site and have review meetings on different projects.
Several live projects, each with its own review cycle and its own set of follow-ups.
3What is the most mentally demanding task you perform?
Solve problems, technical problems. Come up with new, innovative ways of solving problems. Birthing new ideas.
This is the work worth protecting. It needs unbroken thinking, not more tabs.
4Describe your job in one sentence.
I am a Creative Director, a builder, an engineer and an entrepreneur.
One person carrying four functions, which is exactly where repeated admin gets expensive.
5What skills help you thrive in what you do?
Analytic and creative mind.
Judgement and imagination. Both are strengthened by information arriving already prepared.
6Which senses do you rely on most? Sight, touch, hearing, speech?
Sight.
Visual work: drawings, plans, designs and sites. Anything that must be seen before it can be judged.
7Where do you lose the most time? Waiting, searching, or switching between apps?
Searching.
Not a speed problem but a findability one. Time goes into locating what is already there.
8What do you write or type most often, and how many a week?
Typing on my phone.
The phone is the main writing surface. That makes every reply slower than it needs to be.
9Which tasks do you dread, or keep pushing to tomorrow?
Reading bulky documents.
Long documents are the predictable delay. They need the few lines that carry a decision pulled to the top.
10What decisions do you repeat from the same kind of information?
Design approvals.
The same review, applied again and again. A consistent checklist would shorten each pass.
11Which apps do you use daily, and which one frustrates you?
I use KingsChat daily. The platform for learning how to use AI is quite frustrating.
The frustration is with learning the tools themselves, not with the idea of using them.
12What must never leave your control or be shared with a tool?
Financials.
A clear line drawn in advance. Money stays with the person, and the tools work outside it.
13If you gained five hours a week, where would those hours go?
Learning something new.
The freed hours would go back into growth. That is the whole point of lifting the weight.

One reading note
